# Problem

Most renovators are first-timers with no roadmap: they don't know where to start, how to sequence work, or which tradespeople have the right experience. When can decorators paint after winter plastering—two weeks or two months? Should you finish upstairs rooms before removing that kitchen load-bearing wall, or will structural movement crack everything you just completed? Even experienced renovators struggle to estimate costs and timelines accurately.
Renovating older properties is harder still. Online advice conflicts, quotes are ambiguous and compare different scopes, and specialist knowledge like treating damp without trapping moisture, asbestos protocols, breathable materials for period buildings isn't accessible to homeowners. You've remortgaged for that extension, but does the quote include foundations, drainage, Building Control fees? Does "new flooring" cover subfloor repairs and removing the old floor, or will discovering rotten joists triple the cost? By the time you discover what's missing, you're living in the spare room, you've ripped out your kitchen, and stopping isn't an option. Vague specifications make comparison impossible, leaving renovators vulnerable to overruns they can't afford and disputes they can't win.

# Solution: The Clerk for the Modern Renovator

Structa is a mobile and web ecosystem that transforms the chaotic, high-stakes process of home renovation into a coordinated, predictable journey. By combining personal housing document RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) with Agentic AI and visual planning tools, we provide every homeowner with an intelligent planning partner that understands their specific home and guides every decision with financial clarity.

**From 'No Idea Where to Start' → Context-Aware Project Intelligence**

Instead of generic, conflicting advice found online, Structa uses the full context of your specific home profile and uploaded surveys to guide your project.

- **Intelligent Planning**: The AI understands complex technical dependencies—such as ensuring structural work is completed before plastering, or that electrical rough-in happens before plaster-boarding—and creates a sequenced project plan that respects these constraints.
- **Hazard Identification**: The AI analyses your surveys and photos to flag potential issues (suspected asbestos, structural concerns, planning permission requirements) before you start work, preventing costly surprises mid-project.
- **Instant Property Intelligence**: Upload property listings, planning documents, architect drawings, and surveys—the AI transforms static PDFs into a ready-to-use workspace. Room dimensions auto-populate, floor plans scale to traceable backgrounds, and your editor opens with everything needed for markup. Hours of measuring, decoding, and transcribing reduce to seconds—you start with drawings to scale and complete property context.

**From Spreadsheet Chaos → Live Budget Control**

We replace scattered spreadsheets with a live financial tracker that shows your runway, forecasts costs, and keeps you on budget.

- **Real-Time Visibility**: Track actual spend against budget, see your remaining runway, and forecast completion costs as your project evolves.
- **Automated Market Pricing**: The agentic AI proactively searches the web to price specific fixings and furnishings, ensuring your budget reflects current market rates.

**From Vague Descriptions → Professional Visual Specifications**

Our integrated floor plan markup tool lets you communicate exactly what you want with precision that prevents costly misunderstandings.

- **Simple Room Planning**: Upload your Rightmove listing or planning documents. The AI extracts room dimensions to pre-populate scaled floor plans. Use our intuitive editor to mark up changes—new sockets, relocated radiators, moved fixtures.
- **Visual Clarity for Quotes**: Drag and drop electrical, plumbing, and fixture symbols onto your floor plan. Color-code changes (green=new, red=remove, yellow=move). Export professional diagrams that tradespeople can quote from accurately.
- **Scale-Accurate Markup**: Built-in measurement tools ensure your plans are to scale. Tradespeople receive drawings with precise dimensions and specifications—eliminating the "I thought you meant..." conversations.

**From Quote Ambiguity → Informed Hiring Decisions**

The AI helps you evaluate contractor estimates by providing context and scope validation.

- **Demand-Led Professional Network**: When you need quotes, the AI emails local tradespeople with a comprehensive Project Pack containing your house profile, detailed job specifications with visual floor plans, and target timeline—eliminating their qualification calls and admin overhead.
- **Intelligent Tradesperson Matching**: The AI only contacts tradespeople who work with your property type—ensuring your Victorian terrace gets a heritage plasterer who uses breathable lime on solid walls, not a new-build plasterer who's never heard of hygroscopic materials and will insist modern gypsum "works fine everywhere."
- **Smart Quote Analysis**: The AI benchmarks quotes against UK trade standards (via sources like Checkatrade) and aggregated platform data, helping you identify outliers and understand what's reasonable for your area. This intelligence improves as more renovators use Structa in your region.

# Market

The UK home renovation market is undergoing a fundamental shift. Rising property prices have pushed first-time buyers toward older, more affordable properties that need work—[**78% of UK housing stock predates 1980**](https://www.hbf.co.uk/news/housing-horizons/), and [**21% is pre-1919**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Housing_in_the_United_Kingdom). These period properties require specialist knowledge about solid walls, damp management, and heritage construction that's historically been locked away in professional expertise.

Yet most renovators manage projects themselves to save on professional fees. Households spend [**£60 billion annually** on repair, maintenance, and improvement of housing stock, with **£40 billion contracted out** to professionals](https://barbour-abi.com/home-improvement-index-2024/). They're attempting high-stakes, complex projects—averaging [**£25,000-£75,000**](https://www.hillarys.co.uk/static/home-renovation-statistics/)—with tools never designed for renovation: Pinterest for inspiration, Excel for budgets, Google for conflicting advice, and scattered WhatsApp chats with tradespeople.

[**341,068 first-time buyers**](https://www.finder.com/uk/mortgages/first-time-buyer-statistics) purchased homes in 2024, increasingly choosing older properties for affordability and location, then renovating to add value.

**The timing is right:** AI has reached the capability to understand technical documents, reason about dependencies, and provide context-specific guidance. The technology to transform static PDFs into intelligent workspaces, match tradespeople to specific job requirements, and benchmark quotes against market data exists now in ways it didn't three years ago.

Current solutions fall into three categories, none adequate:

- **Generic project management tools** (Asana, Trello) lack renovation-specific intelligence
- **Professional services** (architects, quantity surveyors) are cost-prohibitive for most projects
- **DIY approach** (YouTube, forums, guesswork) leaves renovators vulnerable to expensive mistakes

Structa occupies the white space between "too generic" and "too expensive"—bringing professional-grade intelligence to the DIY-managed renovation market that's been underserved for decades.
